Receptionist Help Guide

This page explains how receptionists can use the Ocean View Resort Reservation System.

1. Login to the System

  1. Open the receptionist login page.
  2. Enter your username and password.
  3. Click Login.
  4. You will be redirected to the receptionist dashboard.

2. Add New Reservation

  1. Click Add Reservation from the dashboard.
  2. Enter guest details such as:
    • Guest Name
    • Phone Number
    • Email
    • Room Type
    • Room Number
    • Check-in Date
    • Check-out Date
  3. The system will check room availability.
  4. Click Save Reservation to confirm booking.

3. View Booking Details

  1. Click View Booking Details.
  2. All rooms will be displayed with their booking status.
  3. Booked rooms will show guest details.
  4. Available rooms can be booked immediately.

4. Generate and Print Bill

  1. Click Calculate & Print Bill.
  2. Enter the Reservation Number.
  3. The system will calculate:
    • Total nights
    • Room charges
    • Tax
    • Total bill amount
  4. Click Print Bill to print the invoice.

5. Checkout Guest

  1. After printing the bill, click Checkout.
  2. The reservation will be removed from the system.
  3. The room becomes available for new reservations.

6. Logout

  1. Click the Logout button on the dashboard.
  2. You will return to the login page.
